# Udemy-Courses-Analysis-Dashboard

This report analyzes Udemy's 2011-2017 course performance using KPIs and trends from a Power BI dashboard.
This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of Udemy courses across key dimensions like subject, pricing, reviews, and engagement metrics. Below are the key takeaways

##Key Insights
-Course Accessibility:

  -A massive 91.57% of the courses are paid, while only 8.43% are free.

-Top Subjects by Lectures:

  -Web Development leads with 42.96% of total lectures, followed by Business Finance (26.27%).

-Course Distribution:

  -Web Development also dominates the total number of courses, followed closely by Business Finance.

 ##Review Metrics
-Total Reviews: 575K

-Highest Review Spike: 2016 saw peak engagement with 90K reviews in Q1.

-Review by Level:

  -Most reviews are for All Levels (404K).

  -Beginner (129K), Intermediate (39K), and Expert (2K) levels follow.

##Pricing Overview
-Max Course Price: $200

-Average Price: $66

##Engagement Metrics
-Average Number of Reviews per Course: 156.26

-Average Rating: 4.09

-Total Lectures Across All Courses: 148K

 ##Summary
Udemy’s platform is dominated by paid content with a strong focus on Web Development and Business Finance. User engagement peaked in 2016 and has since tapered. Beginner-friendly content shows strong review numbers, suggesting high demand at the entry level.
